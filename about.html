(01)About us

A New Maldenfirm, since 2014.

KY Accountancy provides a complete range of accounting services to private individuals and small to medium-sized businesses in the United Kingdom.

We’re based on the High Street in New Malden, home to London’s Korean community, and serve clients across London and the UK. Since 2014 we’ve grown into one of the fastest-growing accountancy firms in south-west London, built on good relationships and word of mouth.

Our accountants have worked across many industries, in finance departments from small businesses to multinational headquarters. We work with sole traders, limited companies, foreign-owned businesses, cross-border e-commerce sellers and charities — in English, Chinese and Korean.
