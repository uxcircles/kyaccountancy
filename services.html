01

Limited companies

A limited company is a separate legal entity from its shareholders, which gives you the protection of limited liability. Some customers and suppliers also prefer to deal with a company rather than a sole trader. We look after the whole compliance calendar so you can focus on growing the business.

What we do for you

  • Company incorporation
  • HMRC tax registrations
  • Bookkeeping
  • Payroll and RTI
  • Workplace pensions
  • VAT returns (Making Tax Digital)
  • Corporation Tax returns (CT600)
  • Year-end accounts for Companies House
  • Confirmation statements
  • Management accounts

Good to know

Private company accounts are due at Companies House 9 months after the year end. Corporation Tax is payable 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period, and the CT600 return is due within 12 months. Directors and people with significant control now also need to verify their identity with Companies House — we can help with that too.

02

Self-employed & sole traders

If your business is relatively simple, registering as self-employed is the easiest and most cost-effective way to start. There's no company law to worry about and no annual accounts to file — if you're not VAT-registered, your main obligation is the Self Assessment return, due by 31 January.

What we do for you

  • UTR registration
  • HMRC tax registrations
  • Bookkeeping
  • Payroll
  • Workplace pensions
  • VAT returns
  • Self Assessment tax return
  • Year-end financial statements
  • Making Tax Digital for Income Tax
  • Management accounts

Making Tax Digital is here

Since 6 April 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000 must keep digital records and send quarterly updates to HMRC. The threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028. We'll set you up with compatible software and file the updates for you.

03

Amazon & online sellers

Selling on Amazon and other marketplaces brings VAT obligations from the very first sale. Overseas businesses that hold stock in the UK have no VAT registration threshold, and storing goods in an EU warehouse usually means registering in that country too. We register you, file the returns and keep you compliant as you expand into new markets.

What we do for you

  • UK VAT registration
  • UK VAT returns
  • EU VAT registration
  • EU One Stop Shop (OSS) returns
  • Monthly or quarterly EU VAT returns
  • German annual VAT return
  • Management reports

Current thresholds

UK: £90,000 for UK-established businesses; no threshold for overseas sellers. EU: a single €10,000 threshold for cross-border sales to consumers across all member states, reported through the One Stop Shop. The old country-by-country distance-selling thresholds were abolished in July 2021.

04

Charities

  • Gift Aid claims
  • Bookkeeping

  • Annual return to the Charity Commission
  • Year-end financial statements
05

Personal & property tax

Our aim is to save you time and money. Good tax planning can reduce your liability significantly — whether you rent out a flat, are selling a property, have income from abroad or think you may have overpaid.

What we do for you

  • Income tax returns
  • Rental income for landlords
  • Capital Gains Tax
  • 60-day property CGT returns
  • Tax refunds
  • Tax credit and allowance claims
  • Personal tax planning

Selling a UK home?

If Capital Gains Tax is due on the sale of UK residential property, you must report and pay it within 60 days of completion — separately from your annual tax return.
