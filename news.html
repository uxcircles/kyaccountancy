Tax changes worth knowing about

Self Assessment6 April 2026

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax has started

From 6 April 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000 must keep digital records and send HMRC quarterly updates. The threshold falls to £30,000 in April 2027 and £20,000 in April 2028. Talk to us about getting set up.

Read on GOV.UK
Limited companies18 November 2025

Companies House identity verification for directors

New directors and people with significant control must now verify their identity with Companies House. Existing directors verify when the company files its next confirmation statement. We can guide you through it.

Read on GOV.UK
VAT1 January 2023

HMRC’s points-based VAT penalties and interest

Late VAT returns now earn penalty points, with a £200 fine once you reach the threshold, and late payments attract separate penalties and interest. Filing and paying on time matters more than ever.
